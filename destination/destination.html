Location

Preah Sihanouk, Cambodia

From Mainland

~25–30 km

Travel Time

2 – 3 hrs

Best Season

Nov – Apr

Gateway Village

Koh Touch

About the Island

Cambodia's largest island,
still mostly jungle

Koh Rong is the largest island of Sihanoukville with the white-sand beaches and warm, clear water. Koh Touch, the main village with most restaurants, dive shops, and moto rentals.

The interior is dense jungle, with a short trail leading to a small waterfall. Beaches range from lively (Long Set at Koh Rong Community).

Getting There

01

Reach Sihanoukville

Bus from Phnom Penh in Sihanoukville, or travel cross province (2-3 hrs by bus).

02

Board the ferry

Speedboats leave from the Sihanoukville terminal daily. Operators include Speed Ferry Cambodia and GTVC.

SPEEDBOAT 45MIN

03

Arrive at Koh Touch

Land at Koh Rong Community, the island's main hub. Most hotels offer pickup; moto are rentable right at the there.

The Day time schedule & Destination

Six stops, plotted against the actual time schedule from 10am landing to the last boat trip at Koh Rong.

10:00AMSihanoukville
11:00AMLong Set Beach
11:20AMKoh Touch
1:00PMBoat Trip
5:00PMSunset Beach
8:00PMReturn Boat

11:00AM — BEACH

Long Set Beach

A short walk from the viewing sand, wave of water calm feeling beachfront there.

11:00AM — ARRIVAL

Koh Rong Community

Land in Koh Touch village. Breakfast, moto rental.

1:00PM — WATER

Snorkeling & Diving

Half-day boat trip in Koh Rong, pick up the tourists in Koh Touch and Koh Rong, and stop at Koh Touch, with a lot of activities along the way.

5:00PM — BOAT

Sunset Beach

West-facing coast, the island's best sunset. Rent a kayak or just the view of sunset on the boat trip.

7:30PM — BOAT

Return Boat

Boat trip will start at 1PM and return at 8:00 after the dinner on the boat.

Good to Know

If you go

Koh Rong rewards slowing down, calm, and relax feeling. Give it more than a single overnight if you can.

Best time Nov–May, dry season, calm seas for the crossing
Get in Speedboat from Sihanoukville (~40–50 min) to the island
Bring Cash just in case slow internet connection
Don't miss swimsuit, sun glasses, goggle
